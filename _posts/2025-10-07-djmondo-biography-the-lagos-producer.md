**DjMondo**, born **Alaile Monday Oluwaseyi**, is a Nigerian record producer, disc jockey, and songwriter from Lagos State. He is known for blending **Afrobeats, Hip-Hop, and Dancehall** into a unique Afro-fusion sound that continues to attract attention across the streaming space.

### Early Life & Background

DjMondo hails from Ondo State, Nigeria. He was born in Bariga, Lagos, and later moved to Ojota where he began his music journey at the age of 18. Before fully focusing on music, he worked as a graphics designer and computer instructor.

### Music Career

DjMondo has built a reputation as one of the versatile producers in the Nigerian music scene. His production style fuses catchy melodies, layered percussion, and powerful basslines — creating sounds that work both on the dancefloor and in personal listening.

Some of his notable works and collaborations include tracks featuring artists such as Naira Marley, Dakushy, SY Easy Nigga, and others.

He also runs his own platform **DjMondo Music**, a straight-talk music media focused on honest artist biographies, music reviews, industry insights, and support for upcoming talent.

### Why DjMondo Stands Out

- Versatile production style across Afrobeats, Hip-Hop, and Dancehall  
- Strong presence on Audiomack and other streaming platforms  
- Consistent branding and engagement with fans  
- Focus on documenting real stories in African music  

> “I make music for the dancefloor and the heart — sounds that move bodies and tell stories.”  
> — **DjMondo**
