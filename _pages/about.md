**DjMondo Music** is a straight-talk music platform dedicated to delivering honest, in-depth breakdowns of artists, music tools, industry trends, and the culture shaping today’s sound — with a strong focus on **Afrobeats** and African music.

Founded by **DjMondo** (Alaile Monday Oluwaseyi), a Nigerian record producer, disc jockey, and songwriter.
